Maps

Each room uses one of these settings, with its own humans, water, obstacles and dangers. Maps are generated from a seed, so they are never exactly the same.

▶ Play free

Elm Street

A quiet neighbourhood... too quiet

A suburban neighbourhood with grid streets, houses, fences and a park with a pond. Teens and neighbours stroll around without a care… until night falls.

Transylvania

The castle awaits its lord

A valley below the castle, with cobbled roads and a river you can only cross over wooden bridges. Villagers, priests and maids: dinner is served.

Camp Serene Lake

Nobody came back from the night shift

Cabins among the pines, a bonfire that is the only light for miles and a lake where something moves beneath the water. Campers and counselors with flashlights… and a full moon.

Witch's Swamp

The cauldron has not gone out in centuries

Pools of black water joined by mud paths and, in the middle, the witch’s dry hut. The muddy trails slow down anyone who does not know the swamp.

Banks of the Nile

Something moves beneath the water

A river full of crocodiles that bite everyone, pyramids, a sphinx and a sandworm lurking under the sand. Tourists, archaeologists and careless villagers.

Jurassic Jungle

The expedition never came back from the temple

A shallow river with a current, a deep lagoon, a ruined temple and the expedition camp. Careful: the dinosaurs hunt here too.
