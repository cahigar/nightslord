Lilith

Succubus · Summoner

Lilith is one of the 30 monsters you can pick in Mooonsters. Hunt humans, level up and evolve at levels 5, 10 and 15.

Backstory

Lilith has broken more hearts than anyone in history, and not figuratively. She has a collection of admirers who follow her everywhere, write her poems and fight for her, and she is a little tired of none of them knowing how to cook. She says love is the strongest magic; her enemies say that is cheating. She wants to be Lord of the Night because a queen needs a whole kingdom to adore her… and a few people to make dinner.

Abilities

⚔Basic attack: Stolen kiss
✦Passive — Steals life with every hit. Humans she hits don't die: they fall in love and fight for her against hunters and monsters.
QLove arrow — Throws a heart that damages, pulls the target towards her and dazes it briefly. If it hits a human, they fall in love.
EWings — Flies over obstacles for a few seconds, faster.
RUltimate (level 10): Unleashed Passion — In a large area everyone attacks the nearest target, but never her. Takedowns count for her.

Evolutions

5Devotion — Steals more life and can have 5 lovers (instead of 3), who last longer.
10Unleashed Passion — Unlocks the R ultimate, which charges with kills.
15Broken heart — Love arrow has 2 charges and pierces enemies; flies for longer.
