Howler

Werewolf · Melee

Howler is one of the 30 monsters you can pick in Mooonsters. Hunt humans, level up and evolve at levels 5, 10 and 15.

▶ Play free
  • Health: 130
  • Speed: 205
  • Armor: 5 %
  • Class: Melee

Abilities

⚔Basic attack: Claw
✦Passive — Wide claw swipe that hits several enemies.
QCharge — Rush forward, damaging everything you touch.
EHowl — +30% damage and +20% speed for 5 s. Freezes humans with fear.
RUltimate (level 10): Full Moon — 7 s in beast mode: bigger, faster and with more reach. Each takedown extends the moon by 1 s.

Evolutions

5Predator instinct — Wounded prey (below 35% health) leave a trail and chasing them speeds you up.
10Full Moon — Unlocks the R ultimate, which charges with kills.
15Alpha Beast — Charge has 2 charges and Howl marks its victims as Prey: your first claw deals extra damage.
