The Necromancer

Lord of bones · Summoner

The Necromancer is one of the 30 monsters you can pick in Mooonsters. Hunt humans, level up and evolve at levels 5, 10 and 15.

▶ Play free
  • Health: 90
  • Speed: 200
  • Armor: 0 %
  • Class: Summoner

Abilities

⚔Basic attack: Dark orb (ranged)
✦Passive — Summoner: shoots balls of dark magic and raises skeletons that follow him. He heals a little with every kill his skeletons make.
QRaise bones — Summons a skeleton warrior or archer (max. 3). Sometimes a lightning-fast skeleton dog comes out.
EMarch of the dead — 4 s: he and his skeletons run and attack faster. He levitates and can cross water.
RUltimate (level 10): Ossuary Portals — 7 s: giant bone fists and feet fall from the sky around the chosen spot, crushing and pushing back enemies.

Evolutions

5Last spell — Raise bones gets 2 charges. When he dies, after 1 s he returns for 4.5 s as a motionless ghost and wields a huge burning ray until he disintegrates.
10Ossuary Portals — Unlocks the R ultimate, which charges with kills.
15Bone army — Up to 5 skeletons at once, 30% bigger and with more health.
